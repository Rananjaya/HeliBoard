# Code of Conduct

RCBBoard is a small personal project. If you take part, please:

- be respectful and friendly, also when you disagree,
- give feedback on the work, not the person,
- be patient with newcomers and with people writing in a language that is not their first,
- respect other people's privacy.

Harassment, insults, discrimination and publishing other people's private information are not welcome.

I may remove comments or contributions, or block people, who do not follow these rules. If you need to tell me about a problem, use a contact option on my GitHub profile: https://github.com/Rananjaya
